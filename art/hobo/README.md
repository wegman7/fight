# Hobo model

The drunk hobo (`Character::Hobo`): a drunken-fist brawler in a ragged brown overcoat
over a grimy shirt, torn trousers and worn boots, with a few days' stubble, lank hair
and a big drinker's nose. He carries a green glass bottle in his near hand and pulls a
beat-up shopping cart out of thin air for his side special. Rigged and animated in
Blender and exported to `godot/models/hobo.glb`. His moveset is in the sim
(`sim/src/character.rs`, frame data at the end of `sim/src/moves.rs`; the root README
lists the moves).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `build_hobo.py` | Generates the whole asset from code: mesh, armature, every animation, the two coat textures. Saves `hobo.blend` and exports the `.glb`. |
| `hobo.blend` | The Blender file. Open it to look at or edit the model by hand. |
| `export_hobo.py` | Exports `hobo.blend` to `godot/models/hobo.glb` (same glTF settings as the chef). |
| `hobo_coat.png` | The coat's base-colour texture: the mottled, stained coat colour, generated by `make_textures()`. |
| `hobo_coat_rough.png` | The coat's roughness texture: noise for its weathered, uneven finish. |

```sh
blender --background --factory-startup --python art/hobo/build_hobo.py      # rebuild from the script
blender --background art/hobo/hobo.blend --python art/hobo/export_hobo.py   # or export hand edits
godot --headless --path godot --import                                        # then reimport
```

As with the chef, rebuilding from the script overwrites hand edits to `hobo.blend`.
Choose one approach per change.

## Built on the chef

He stands on the chef's skeleton (same joints, 1.2 m tall), so `build_hobo.py` imports
`art/fighter/build_fighter.py` for the metaball shapes (`Blob`), the skinning helpers and
the pose helpers (`guard`, `crouch`, `walk_key`, `leg_ik`, `planted`, `with_`, `AIR`,
`FLINCH`). Changing those in the chef's script changes the hobo too, so rebuild both.
The chef's pose convention applies (see
[`art/fighter/README.md`](../fighter/README.md#pose-convention-in-build_fighterpy)).

The rules in [`art/fighter/README.md`](../fighter/README.md#rules-the-game-relies-on)
apply here too, except for the move names: the hobo needs the shared state and dodge
animations plus one per hobo move (`Character::Hobo.moves()` — all 27 of them, each
prefixed `hobo_`, since he doesn't share the chef's grab). The game logs an `ERROR` at
startup for any it can't find. `godot/models/hobo.glb.import` must keep `animation/fps=60`.

## The props

- **The `bottle` bone** (child of `forearm.R`) runs along +X through the right fist,
  like the samurai's katana. It's a multi-part prop: a red cap, a green glass neck,
  shoulder and body, and a paper label wrapped round the body. `bottle_in(pose, facing)`
  shows it in the fist and aims its neck (`0` = forward, `90` = up); every other action
  keys its scale to 0, so it never lingers. It shows during `hobo_bottle_swing`,
  `hobo_up_heave` and `hobo_bottle_throw` (where it vanishes on frame 12 as the sim
  fires the projectile).
- **The `cart` bone** (child of `hips`, in front of him) carries the shopping cart:
  a wire basket of two hoops and slanting bars, a red handle, four legs, four caster
  wheels and an under-tray. `cart_in(pose)` shows it, keyed only during
  `hobo_cart_charge`; everywhere else its scale is 0.

## The coat's grime

The coat's `Coat` material uses two textures generated by `make_textures()` — a coarse
value noise baked to `hobo_coat.png` (the base colour, mottled with darker grime stains
and lighter worn patches) and `hobo_coat_rough.png` (its roughness), so the grimy,
weathered finish survives into the exported glb. The seams are thin stitch tubes sewn
onto the coat by ray casting, and darker `Grime` patches are placed at the hem and
elbows. `Body` is the shirt underneath, which gets the player's colour and state tints
in game; the coat keeps its own colour.

## How it's built

- **Cloth and flesh** are metaball surfaces, one per piece: the gaunt head (hollow
  cheeks, big drinker's nose), the lank hair, a stubble layer hugging the jaw and chin,
  the ragged coat body (broad shoulders, a long open front flaring to a torn hem) and
  each baggy sleeve (with its fold hanging under the arm), the grimy shirt, each torn
  trouser leg (a hole at the knee) and each worn boot.
- **Details** are bmesh parts placed by ray casting or built directly: the narrowed eyes,
  scowling brows and a red nose tip, the coat's stitch seams and grime stains, the
  bottle and the cart.
- **Hands** have four separate fingers and a thumb (the chef's are loose fists), spread
  for grabbing and slapping.
- **Materials:** `Body` (the shirt) gets the player's colour in game. `Coat`, `Grime`,
  `Skin`, `Lip`, `Hair`, `Stubble`, `Eye`, `Iris`, `Pupil`, `Pants`, `Shoe`, `Bottle`,
  `Label`, `Cap`, `Cart`, `CartWheel` and `CartTrim` keep their own.
